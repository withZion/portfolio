About

how I think about building

I build systems that watch, decide, act and report back. Give me a process someone repeats every day, and I'll find the rules inside it and turn them into software that runs without them.

My rule is decisions in code, not in prompts. AI is brilliant at conversation and explanation, so that's where I use it. The decisions that matter go to deterministic, tested code you can audit.

I care about what a system refuses to do as much as what it does. No silent failures, no duplicate alerts, no guessed data. If something breaks, it tells you.

And it has to look good. The interface is part of the product.

Away from the keyboard I'm reading, playing chess or watching football. Lately I'm sharpening the product and marketing side of shipping too.
